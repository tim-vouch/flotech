What Is A Power Flush?

A power flush is a deep cleaning process that removes sludge, rust and debris from your central heating system using specialist equipment and cleaning chemicals. Over time, these contaminants can build up inside radiators, pipework and boilers, reducing efficiency and placing extra strain on your heating system.

The power flushing process helps restore proper circulation within the system, allowing radiators to heat more evenly and your boiler to operate more efficiently.

At Flotech Heating & Plumbing Ltd, we use professional power flushing equipment to safely clean your system while minimising disruption to your home.

Signs Your Heating System May Need A Power Flush

There are several common signs that your heating system may benefit from a power flush. If left untreated, sludge buildup can place unnecessary pressure on your boiler and heating components, potentially leading to expensive repairs or reduced system lifespan. Look for these signs:

  • Cold spots on radiators
  • Radiators are taking longer to heat up
  • Noisy boiler or heating system
  • Dirty or discoloured water
  • Frequent boiler breakdowns
  • Reduced heating efficiency
  • Some radiators are not heating properly
  • Increased energy bills
